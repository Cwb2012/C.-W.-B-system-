About C.W.B

Learn about our company, our work and our commitment to quality.

Camel Work and Business Company

C.W.B is a business based in Bujumbura, Burundi, focused on automotive paint, spare parts, vehicle finishing and garage-related services.

We aim to provide practical solutions for vehicle owners and business customers while building professional and lasting relationships.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality automotive products and services, meet our customers' needs, and build lasting relationships through professional work, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Commitment

We believe that quality, reliability and professional service are important foundations for business growth. Our goal is to help customers find solutions for their automotive and business needs.

  • Focus on quality and reliability
  • Professional automotive solutions
  • Customer-focused service
  • Business organization and planning
  • Commitment to continuous improvement

Our slogan: Better Quality is our priority.
